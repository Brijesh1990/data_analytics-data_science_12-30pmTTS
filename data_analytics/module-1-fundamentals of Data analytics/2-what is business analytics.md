# What is Business Analytics?

1. Business analytics uses data and statistical methods to understand past business performance.
2. It helps identify patterns, trends, and relationships in business data.
3. It supports better decision-making through reports, dashboards, forecasting, and optimization.


# Roles of Business Analytics

1. Collect, clean, and prepare business data.
2. Analyze sales, customer, finance, marketing, and operational data.
3. Create dashboards and reports to visualize key performance indicators.
4. Identify trends, risks, opportunities, and areas for improvement.
5. Build forecasting and predictive models for future business outcomes.
6. Use tools such as Excel, SQL, Python, Power BI, and Tableau.
7. Work with management and stakeholders to solve business problems.
8. Recommend data-driven strategies and measure their impact.
